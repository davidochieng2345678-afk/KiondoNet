What drives prices up

  • Material. Leather soles cost more than rubber. Suede, nubuck and exotic skins cost more than smooth leather.
  • Parts. Branded zips (YKK, Riri) and quality buckles cost more.
  • Damage extent. A worn sole is quick; a cracked upper is hours of work.
  • Turnaround. Same-day service adds 30–50%.
  • Location. Some areas charge more, though our pricing is consistent across Nairobi.

What keeps prices down

  • Bulk orders. 20+ pairs get per-pair discounting.
  • Bundled services. A resole plus polish is cheaper together than separately.
  • Regular customers. We look after people who come back.

What you should not skimp on

Cheap repairs are the most expensive kind. A glue-and-go resole lasts 3 months; a properly stitched resole lasts 3 years. If the quote is dramatically cheaper than everyone else, ask what's being left out.
