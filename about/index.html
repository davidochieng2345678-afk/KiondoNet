About Leather Clinic Nairobi

A workshop built on one belief: good leather deserves a second life.

Our Story

Leather Clinic Nairobi started with a simple frustration — watching quality shoes and jackets thrown away because a zip failed or a sole wore through. In a city where mitumba leather is everywhere and genuine leather goods are an investment, that made no sense.

So we built a proper workshop. Not a roadside kiosk with glue and a hammer, but a bench with real tools, proper threads, industrial machines and craftsmen who take pride in invisible repairs.

Our Craft

From resoling a beloved pair of Oxfords to bringing a mitumba leather jacket back to life, every item is treated as if it were our own.

Leatherwork

Hand-stitching, machine stitching, edge finishing, skiving and structural repair.

Colour & Finishing

Professional dyes, pigments and topcoats matched to your original colour.

Soles & Heels

Rubber, TPR, leather and combination soles cut and fitted on site.

Hardware

Zips, sliders, clasps, buckles, rivets and D-rings in brass, nickel and gunmetal.

Our Commitment

  • Quality. Premium materials and time-tested techniques — no shortcuts.
  • Transparency. Clear pricing, honest assessments, no surprise charges.
  • Convenience. Pickup and delivery across Nairobi and surrounding areas.
  • Sustainability. Every repair keeps leather out of a landfill in Dandora.
