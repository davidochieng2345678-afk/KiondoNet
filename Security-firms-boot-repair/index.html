The Problem With Boots

Security guards walk 8–15 km a day in their boots. Soles wear through in months, heels collapse, steel toe caps get exposed. Replacing every pair every 6–12 months is a major line item in any security firm's operations budget.

How We Help

  • Resoling — new rubber or PU soles fitted and stitched, extending boot life by 12+ months.
  • Heel rebuild — reinforces the heel so it doesn't collapse.
  • Steel toe repair — patches to cover exposed toe caps.
  • Upper restitching — reinforces seams that split with constant flexing.
  • Conditioning — keeps leather supple so it doesn't crack and split.

Why It Works

Each rebuilt boot costs roughly 30–50% of a new pair, and typically lasts almost as long as a new one. For a firm with 200 guards, repairing 300 boots a year instead of replacing them can save several million shillings — and you keep the same broken-in boots on the job.
