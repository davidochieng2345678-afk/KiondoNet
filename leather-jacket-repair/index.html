What We Can and Can't Do

We can

  • Replace any zip, including heavy-duty and two-way zips.
  • Rebuild a lining from scratch using your jacket's original pattern.
  • Fill deep cracks and recolour the entire jacket.
  • Take in a jacket that has become too large.
  • Shorten sleeves and reattach original cuffs.

We can't

  • Make a jacket significantly bigger — there's no extra material.
  • Restore leather that has fully rotted or torn into pieces.
  • Match bonded or faux leather colours perfectly, since these don't absorb dye.
