Zip & Clasp Replacement

Zips, sliders, clasps, turn locks and magnetic closures — replaced with proper hardware.

Get a Free Quote

What We Replace

  • Main zips — the zips that open the main compartment.
  • Secondary zips — interior pockets, laptop sleeves, side pockets.
  • Sliders only — when the teeth are fine but the slider has worn out.
  • Magnetic clasps — flush magnetic closures on flap bags.
  • Turn locks — push-lock or twist-lock closures on briefcases.
  • Press studs — snap closures on flaps and straps.
  • Buckles — decorative and functional buckles on straps.

Zip Options

  • Ykk, Riri, Lampo — the quality brands we usually fit for durability.
  • Heavy-duty zips — larger teeth for a bag that gets packed tightly.
  • Antique / brass / nickel / gunmetal / black — matched to your bag's existing hardware.
  • Water-resistant zips — for outdoor and travel bags.

Why It's Worth It

A broken zip is often the only thing wrong with an otherwise perfect bag. Replacing it costs a fraction of the bag's value and gets you another decade of use. We never use plastic replacement zips — they fail within a year.
