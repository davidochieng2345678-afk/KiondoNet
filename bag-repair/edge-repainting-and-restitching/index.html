What Is Edge Painting?

Most leather bags have a painted edge coat along the leather's cut edges — the sides of the straps, the edges of the gussets, the outline of the flap. This coat wears off over time, leaving raw, lighter leather exposed. Repainting restores a clean, finished edge that matches the bag's original look.

When Edges Need Repainting

  • Edge coat is peeling, flaking or worn away.
  • Edges look lighter than the rest of the bag.
  • The bag looks tired even when cleaned.
  • Edges have absorbed water and gone darker in patches.
  • You're restoring an old bag and want the finish to look new.

Restitching

Loose, broken or worn stitching is the second most common visual problem on older bags. We restitch:

  • Straps where the original stitch line has failed.
  • Flaps and top seams.
  • Gusset seams where side panels meet the body.
  • Pocket edges and interior linings.

We match the thread colour and stitch length to the original so the repair is invisible.
