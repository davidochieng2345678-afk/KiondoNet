Why Travel Bags Fail

Travel bags take more abuse than any other leather item. They're dragged, thrown, packed to capacity, exposed to rain and airport handling. The weak points reveal themselves quickly: handles, zip sliders, base corners and where the strap attaches.

Common Repairs

  • Handle rebuild — full leather handle replacement with reinforced attachment.
  • Heavy-duty zip replacement — larger teeth, stronger slider for a bag that gets packed tight.
  • Base reinforcement — a new leather panel or studs on the bottom.
  • Strap attachment repair — reinforced leather loops, new rivets.
  • Corner replacement — full leather corner sections replaced.
  • Lining rebuild — including interior pockets and dividers.
  • Wheeled bag telescopic handle repair — where applicable.

Reinforcement Options

For a duffel that gets heavy use, we recommend:

  • Rivets added at handle attachment points.
  • Leather patches under handles to spread load.
  • Metal base studs to lift the leather off wet surfaces.
  • Double stitching at high-stress seams.
