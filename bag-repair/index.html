Common Bag Problems We Fix

  • Handle detached from the bag body — usually a rivet or stitch failure.
  • Zip slider broken or teeth misaligned.
  • Corners worn through from daily contact with surfaces.
  • Lining torn at the seams, especially around the laptop compartment.
  • Edge coating flaking off, leaving raw leather exposed.
  • Shoulder strap stretched or the adjustment buckle snapped.
