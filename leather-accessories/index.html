Why Bother Repairing a Wallet or Belt?

Because a good leather wallet or belt that has moulded to you is hard to replace. The leather softens, the card slots loosen to exactly the right tension, and the belt sits at the hole you actually use. Restitching a seam costs far less than starting over.
