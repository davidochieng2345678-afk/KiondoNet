Split seams, torn folds and worn-out card slots fixed quickly.

Common Wallet Problems

  • Split seams — the outer stitch line has failed at the fold.
  • Card slots torn — leather worn thin or torn at slot openings.
  • Broken fold — the main fold has cracked from constant opening.
  • Loose snap or stud — press stud no longer holds.
  • Zip failure — on zip wallets and coin pockets.
  • Worn interior lining — fabric or leather lining worn through.

What We Do

  • Restitch split seams by hand, matching original thread.
  • Reinforce card slots with new leather edging.
  • Rebuild the fold with a flexible leather reinforcement.
  • Replace snap studs and zip sliders.
  • Replace interior lining.
  • Condition and finish the leather.

Why Repair a Wallet

A good wallet moulds to your pocket and your card stack. Replacing it means breaking in a new one. If the leather itself is intact and the failures are seams or slots, repair is straightforward and cheap.
