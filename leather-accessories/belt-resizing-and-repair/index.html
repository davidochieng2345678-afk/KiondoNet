Lost weight? Bought a belt that's too long? We shorten leather belts cleanly.

Why Resize a Belt?

A leather belt that's too long tangles and looks sloppy. Belts can be shortened from either end — from the buckle end (preserves the original tip) or the tip end (preserves the buckle attachment). We choose the method that produces the best result based on your belt's construction.

Belt Services We Offer

  • Shortening — cut from the buckle end or tip end, re-finished cleanly.
  • New holes — additional holes punched at your exact spacing.
  • Crack repair — filling and recolouring cracks in the belt leather.
  • Edge refinishing — restoring the belt's painted edges.
  • Restitching — where the belt's stitch line has failed.
  • Reversible belt conversion — fixing or replacing the rotating buckle.

How We Shorten

  1. Measure your exact waist-to-buckle distance with the belt on.
  2. Cut from the agreed end.
  3. Reduce the belt's thickness at the new edge (skiving).
  4. Repaint or re-finish the edge.
  5. Reattach the buckle or re-punch holes as needed.
  6. Condition the finished belt.
