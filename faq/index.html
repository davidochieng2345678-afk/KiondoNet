How long does a repair take?

Most shoe repairs are ready in 1–3 working days. Leather jacket restoration, redyeing or resizing typically takes 3–7 days depending on complexity and drying time. Express same-day service is available on simple jobs for a small premium.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. We offer pickup and delivery across Nairobi and surrounding areas, including Westlands, Upper Hill, CBD, Kilimani, Kileleshwa, Lavington, Parklands, South B, South C, Embakasi, Rongai, Kasarani, Ruiru, Syokimau and Thika Road. Book a pickup →

How much does shoe repair cost in Nairobi?

Resoling starts from KES 1,500, heel replacement from KES 800, and polishing from KES 300. Final pricing depends on material, damage and parts required. See our full price guide or call for an exact quote.

Can you repair mitumba leather jackets?

Absolutely — it is one of our specialities. We handle deep cleaning, crack and peel repair, zip and lining replacement, resizing and full redyeing. A good mitumba jacket plus KES 4,500 of restoration often beats a new jacket costing five times as much.

Can you fix cracked or peeling leather?

Yes. We clean back the damaged area, fill with flexible leather filler, re-colour to match and seal with a protective topcoat. Deep cracks and large peeling areas can be restored, though we will always tell you honestly if the leather is too far gone.

Do you repair safety boots for companies?

Yes. We offer bulk safety boot repair for security firms, construction companies, logistics operations and hospitality groups. See corporate boot repair →

How do I know if my shoes need resoling?

Look for: smooth or worn tread, a hole in the sole, discomfort while walking, the sole separating from the upper, cracks in the sole, or slipping. In Nairobi's climate, leather soles degrade faster than most people expect.

Is it worth repairing leather shoes?

For quality leather shoes — yes, almost always. A well-made pair can last decades with resoling and proper care. Repair typically costs 20–40% of a replacement pair. Read Resole vs Buy New.

Can you make shoes bigger or smaller?

We can stretch shoes up to roughly half a size using professional stretchers, and tighten loose shoes to a limited degree. Structural changes beyond that are not usually economical.

Do you repair suede and nubuck?

Yes. Suede and nubuck need different treatments — dry cleaning rather than wet, and specialist brushes and colourants. Always mention the material when booking.

What payment methods do you accept?

M-Pesa, cash and bank transfer. Corporate accounts can be invoiced on agreed terms.

What if a repair isn't possible?

We will tell you before you spend anything. Our assessment is free, and we would rather turn down a job than return something that will fail in a month.
