Why Repair Instead of Replace

A well-made leather shoe improves with wear. It moulds to your foot, the leather softens, and the welt holds its shape. Resoling costs a fraction of a new pair and keeps all of that. It's also the reason a KES 15,000 pair of shoes can serve you for fifteen years.
