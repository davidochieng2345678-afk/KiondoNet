What Is Resoling?

Resoling replaces the worn sole of a shoe while keeping the upper, insole and structure intact. For quality leather shoes, resoling is what keeps them going for years — the uppers often outlast two or three sets of soles.

When Your Shoes Need Resoling

  • The tread has worn smooth and grip is gone.
  • A hole has formed at the ball of the foot.
  • The sole is separating from the upper.
  • You can feel the ground through the sole when walking.
  • Water soaks through within minutes.

Sole Types We Fit

  • Rubber — best for everyday wear and Nairobi streets. Hard-wearing, quiet, comfortable.
  • TPR — soft and flexible, common on casual and walking shoes.
  • Leather — traditional on welted dress shoes. Elegant but needs more care in rain.
  • Combination — rubber toe and heel with leather mid-section.
  • Vibram-style lug soles — for boots and shoes needing extra grip.

How We Do It

  1. Assess the upper, welt and insole for structural integrity.
  2. Remove the old sole and clean the welt and midsole.
  3. Cut and shape the new sole to your shoe.
  4. Stitch the sole back to the welt (welted shoes) or cement it (cemented shoes).
  5. Trim, burnish and polish the edges.
  6. Finish with a heel and top-piece replacement if needed.
