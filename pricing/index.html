What Affects the Price

  • Material — full-grain leather repairs differently from corrected grain or synthetic.
  • Extent of damage — a worn sole is simpler than a torn upper.
  • Parts required — branded zips, buckles and soles cost more than standard ones.
  • Turnaround — same-day work carries a premium.
  • Volume — bulk and corporate orders are quoted separately. See corporate services →
