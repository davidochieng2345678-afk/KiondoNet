Why Repair Instead of Replace

A pair of safety boots costs KES 4,000–12,000. A full resoling and heel rebuild typically costs 30–50% of that. For firms with dozens or hundreds of staff in the field, repairing boots instead of replacing them saves several million shillings a year — without ever compromising safety.

How to Get Started

  2. We provide per-item rates and a projected turnaround.
  3. We schedule a pickup from your premises.

  5. You're invoiced on agreed terms.
