Privacy Policy

How Leather Clinic Nairobi collects, uses and protects your information.

Information We Collect

  • Your name, phone number and email address when you contact us or book a repair.
  • Photographs of items submitted for assessment.
  • Delivery address and location details when you request pickup and delivery.
  • Basic website analytics (pages visited, device type, approximate location).

How We Use It

  • To assess your item and provide an accurate quote.
  • To carry out the repair and return your item.
  • To contact you about your order.
  • To improve our services and website.

What We Don't Do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not send marketing messages unless you have asked us to.

Photographs of Your Items

We may photograph repaired items for our before-and-after gallery. If you would prefer your item not be published, tell us and we will not use it. We never publish your name or contact details without permission.

Data Storage

Customer records are stored securely and retained only as long as needed to service your repair and meet legal obligations.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and third-party analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing info@leatherclinic.co.ke.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email info@leatherclinic.co.ke or call +254 700 000 000.
